**Wigglet 1.0.1** fixes Wigglets standing still and old sessions hanging around.

- **Wigglets not animating?** Older hooks (from a previous build or Clawd Pet) were treated as connected, so no activity reached the app. Open Wigglet and click **Reconnect** once.
- Sessions you haven't touched for 10 minutes now step aside (change it in Settings). A session waiting on you always stays.
- Ended sessions leave for good; speech bubbles fit their panel.
- Credits to Anthropic, who made Claude, Claude Code and the mascot this is a tribute to. See [TRADEMARKS.md](https://github.com/Icore0/wigglet/blob/main/TRADEMARKS.md).

Installed copies of 1.0.0 update themselves. New here? Download `Wigglet.dmg` below; first launch needs **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (it isn't notarized yet).
